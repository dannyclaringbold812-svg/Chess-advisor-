3. Advisor Highlight
Auto Advisor is ON — the advisor will recommend and highlight YOUR next move in blue-to-red only.
Support Mode is ON — blue-to-red is a recommendation only. You can make any legal move you want.
Advisor safety layer: checks material risk before recommending sacrifices and uses opening-specific guidance in Scholar's Mate positions.
High-Value Piece Safety Gate: before recommending an attack, the advisor checks whether your Queen, Rooks, Bishops or Knights would be left hanging or exposed to a losing exchange. Checkmate can override the gate; ordinary attacks cannot.
Threat Escape Priority: if your Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight is already under serious threat, the advisor prioritises the safest calculated escape — including a safe retreat, defended square, profitable capture, or trade — before looking for an attack.
You can still manually choose the advisor’s blue starting square and red destination square if you want to override the automatic recommendation.
